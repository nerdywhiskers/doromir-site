The promise

Four things we will not do.

  • Move a free feature behind the paywall. Anything you can use today stays usable, in every version after this one.
  • Charge you for your own data. Recording, reading, searching and exporting are never metered.
  • Run ads, trackers, or a data sale. There is nothing to sell and nowhere to sell it from.
  • Strand you. If a paid tier ever shut down, your journal is already on your phone and your export is a plain file.
