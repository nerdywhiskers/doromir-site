Speak your dream before it fades.

An alarm clock and dream journal that wakes you into a voice recorder. Fully private, nothing leaves your phone.

Coming to Android

Capture

Wake up. Start talking.

The alarm takes over the lock screen. One tap starts recording. No unlocking or hunting for an app while the dream fades away.

Journal

Your journal writes itself.

Titles, tags and moods are added for you. Search everything you have ever logged, and watch the motifs start repeating. No complicated charts to fill out, or unscientific mystical predictions.

Insights

Patterns you would never notice.

Every dream becomes a dot, and dreams that resemble each other or share tags show as clusters. Entries also read against sleep duration, REM, deep sleep and activity. The more you use it, the richer the insights become.

* Sleep and Health figures depend on what your phone and any connected watch or tracker can actually record.

Private

Fully Offline and Private.

Your phone is perfectly capable of analysing your dreams using local AI models. Your dreams are never sent anywhere.

* Only supported on newer devices. Subject to your device's privacy settings.

Beyond

Go beyond a Dream Journal.

Doromir can also be used as a smart note-taking app. It supports exporting in multiple formats, and is designed to be modular. Sync it into an Obsidian vault for even more control over your data.

Additional features on the roadmap:

  • Fine-tuned local AI model
  • Fully local journal chat
  • Multi-device sync
  • Multiple journal profiles

The pledge

Nothing leaves your phone.

  • No account or email required.
  • No analytics, no ads, no trackers.
  • No external servers. (Why let OpenAI read your dreams?)
  • Uninstalling deletes everything. There is no other copy.

Availability
