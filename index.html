What it does

Built around the ninety seconds after you wake.

Dream recall collapses within minutes of waking. Every design decision in Doromir serves getting words out of your head before that happens — and keeping them somewhere only you can read.

An alarm that opens into a recorder

The alarm takes over the lock screen with a full-screen wake interface. One tap starts recording — no unlocking, no hunting for an app while the dream evaporates.

Voice capture, transcribed on the device

Speak naturally and stop when you run out. Your phone's own speech recognition turns it into text, with a bundled Whisper model as a fallback. Recordings are deleted after transcription unless you choose to keep them.

Nightstand mode

A black screen, a large dim clock, and a wake target big enough to hit half-asleep in the dark. Park the phone beside the bed and it stops being a phone.

A journal that notices its own patterns

Entries get titles, tags and moods automatically. Search across everything you have logged, filter by date, and watch recurring motifs surface as the journal grows.

Sleep, correlated — only if you ask

Connect Health Connect, or log a night by hand, to see your dreams against sleep duration, REM, deep sleep, HRV and resting heart rate. Read-only, analysed on-device. Decline it and everything else still works.

Optional on-device AI

Doromir can run a small language model locally for richer themes and reflections. Downloading it is your choice, it runs offline once it is there, and your dreams are never uploaded to be analysed.

The pledge

Nothing leaves your phone.

This is not a marketing line with an asterisk. Doromir has no backend to send anything to.

  • No account, no sign-in, no email address required.
  • No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, no third-party trackers.
  • No server — your dreams have nowhere else to go.
  • Transcription, tagging and analysis all run on your device.
  • Health data is read-only, never written back, never uploaded, never sold.
  • Export your whole journal as a file whenever you want it.
  • Uninstalling deletes everything, because there is no copy anywhere else.
