Privacy Policy

Effective 4 August 2026 · applies to the Doromir mobile app and this website

The short version: Doromir has no user account, no cloud sync and no server. Your recordings, transcripts, journal entries and any health data you connect are stored in a database on your own phone and are never transmitted to us or to anyone else. We do not collect analytics, we do not run advertising, and we have no way to read your dreams. Uninstalling the app deletes everything.

1. Who we are

Doromir is a dream-journal and alarm-clock app published by Nerdy Whiskers LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with information on your device, and what — if anything — reaches us. It covers the Doromir mobile app and this website.

2. The architecture, because it determines everything else

Most privacy policies describe what a company does with data it has collected. This one is different because Doromir is built so that we never receive your data in the first place. There is no Doromir account system, no login, no backend service and no cloud storage. The app runs entirely on your device.

That is not a promise about our intentions — it is a property of how the software is built. We could not hand your dream journal to anyone, because we do not have it.

3. What Doromir stores on your device

All of the following is written to local storage on your phone and stays there:

  • Audio recordings of dream notes you capture (see section 5).
  • Transcripts of those recordings.
  • Journal entries, including titles, tags, moods and any text you type or edit.
  • Numeric representations of your entries ("embeddings") used for on-device search and to find recurring themes.
  • Alarms, sounds and app settings.
  • Health and sleep figures, if you choose to connect a health source or enter them by hand (see section 7).

4. What we collect about you

Nothing. To be specific, Doromir contains no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK, no advertising SDK and no third-party trackers. We do not collect device identifiers, usage statistics, diagnostics, contacts, location or an email address. We do not build a profile of you, and there is nothing for us to sell or share, so we do not sell or share it.

5. Microphone and audio recordings

The microphone is used only while you are actively on the capture screen recording a dream note. It is not used at any other time, and the app does not listen in the background.

Recordings are written to local storage on your device and are never uploaded. By default a recording is deleted as soon as it has been transcribed; if you turn on audio retention in Settings, saved recordings are kept on the device until you delete the entry. Discarding a capture deletes its audio immediately.

6. Speech transcription

Transcription happens on your device. Doromir uses your phone's built-in speech recognition where available, and otherwise falls back to a Whisper speech model that ships inside the app itself. Your audio is not sent to Doromir for transcription, because there is nowhere to send it.

Your device's own speech recognition is a component of your operating system and is governed by your device manufacturer's privacy policy. If you would rather not use it, Doromir's bundled offline model works without any system speech service.

7. Health and fitness data

Connecting a health source is entirely optional. If you decline, every other feature of Doromir works normally. You can also enter sleep figures by hand and never grant health access at all.

What Doromir reads

On Android, Doromir requests read-only access to the following Health Connect record types, and nothing else:

  • Sleep sessions — including stages such as REM and deep sleep, to relate dream recall to sleep architecture.
  • Heart rate variability (RMSSD) — an overnight recovery signal.
  • Resting heart rate — an overnight recovery signal.
  • Steps — daytime activity, to see whether activity relates to dream recall.
  • Active calories burned — daytime activity, for the same reason.
  • Exercise sessions — to place workouts against the nights that follow them.

On iOS, should an iOS version be released, the equivalent Apple Health types would be read on the same read-only basis.

What Doromir does with it

Health figures are read onto your device, stored locally alongside your journal and analysed on the device to show reflective correlations — for example, whether your longer dream entries tend to follow nights with more REM sleep. The results are displayed only to you, in the app.

What Doromir never does with it

  • Health data is never transmitted off your device, to us or to anyone else.
  • Health data is never sold, rented or shared with any third party.
  • Health data is never used for advertising or marketing of any kind, by us or by anyone else.
  • Health data is never used to make decisions about you regarding employment, insurance, credit or eligibility for anything.
  • Doromir requests read access only. It never writes to, alters or deletes anything in your health store.
  • Health data is not used to train any machine-learning model, ours or anyone else's.

You can withdraw health access at any time in Health Connect (or your device's Settings). Doing so stops all further reads immediately. Figures already copied into Doromir's local database are removed when you delete the app's data or uninstall it — see Delete your data.

Optional sleep detection using your phone's sensors

On Android, if you have no wearable, you can let Doromir estimate when you slept using your phone's own motion sensors, via the system activity-recognition permission. This runs entirely on-device, stores at most the last few nights' detected sleep windows locally, and is off unless you turn it on. It is not location tracking and Doromir does not request location access.

8. On-device AI, and the only network connection Doromir makes

Doromir can generate titles, tags and reflective themes from your entries. This runs on your device. Depending on your phone, it may use a small model that ships with the app, a system AI service provided by your device, or an optional larger language model.

The one exception to "nothing leaves your phone" is downloading that optional model. If — and only if — you choose to download it, your device connects to Hugging Face (huggingface.co), a third-party model host, to fetch the model file. That connection is an ordinary file download: it reveals to that host the same things any download does, such as your IP address, and is subject to their privacy policy. No part of your journal, recordings, transcripts or health data is included in that request. Once the model is on your device it runs offline, and you can delete it from Settings at any time.

Apart from that optional download, the released Doromir app makes no network requests. It functions fully in airplane mode.

9. Notifications and alarms

Alarms are scheduled and delivered entirely by your device. Doromir uses no push-notification service, so no server is involved and no device token is registered anywhere. Notification content stays on your phone.

10. Permissions Doromir requests, and why

  • Microphone — to record dream notes. Required for voice capture.
  • Notifications — to deliver alarms and reminders.
  • Exact alarms — so an alarm fires at the minute you set rather than whenever the system gets round to it.
  • Full-screen intent — so the alarm can present its wake interface over the lock screen.
  • Display over other apps — optional. Android suppresses a full-screen alarm while the phone is awake and in use; this permission lets the alarm open properly in that case. Doromir draws no overlay windows and the alarm still rings without it.
  • Run at startup — to restore your scheduled alarms after the phone restarts.
  • Foreground service, wake lock and vibration — to keep an alarm ringing and wake the screen.
  • Physical activity — optional, for phone-sensor sleep detection (section 7).
  • Health Connect (read) — optional, for the six record types listed in section 7.

Every permission can be changed at any time from your device's system settings. The optional ones can be declined without losing access to the rest of the app.

11. Sharing and disclosure

We do not share your information with anyone, because we do not have it. There are no third-party processors, no data brokers, no advertising partners and no affiliates receiving anything. If you personally choose to export your journal and send the file somewhere, that is your action and outside this policy.

We could not meaningfully respond to a subpoena or law-enforcement request for your dream journal, because no copy of it exists outside your device.

12. Security

Your data is protected by your device's own security: the screen lock you set and the full-disk encryption that modern Android and iOS apply by default. Doromir's storage is private to the app and not readable by other apps on a non-rooted device.

Because there is no server, there is no server to breach. The corresponding trade-off is real and worth stating plainly: if you lose your phone and have not exported your journal, the data is gone. Doromir cannot recover it for you. Use the export feature if your journal matters to you.

13. Retention and deletion

Doromir keeps your entries on your device until you delete them. There is no retention schedule on our side because we hold nothing. Deleting an entry also deletes its audio file. Uninstalling the app removes everything. Full instructions are on the Delete your data page.

14. Children

Doromir is not directed to children and is not intended for use by anyone under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent where you live, if that is higher). We do not knowingly collect information from children — and in fact collect no information from anyone.

15. Your rights

Privacy laws including the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, export and delete personal data that a company holds about you. In Doromir's case you can exercise all of these directly, without asking us and without waiting:

  • Access — everything is in the app, on your device.
  • Portability — export your entire journal and health snapshots as a file from the Profile screen.
  • Correction — edit any entry directly.
  • Erasure — delete entries individually, or uninstall to remove everything.

We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under the CCPA, and we have never done so.

16. Insights are reflective, not medical

Patterns, themes and correlations shown in Doromir are generated locally as a tool for personal reflection. They are not medical advice, diagnosis, treatment or prediction, and Doromir is not a medical device. If you are concerned about your sleep or your mental health, speak to a qualified health professional. If you are in crisis, contact your local emergency number or a crisis line in your country.

17. This website

This site is a set of static pages. It sets no cookies, includes no analytics and embeds no third-party scripts or fonts. Our host records standard server logs, such as IP addresses of requests, for the purpose of serving the site.

18. Changes to this policy

If Doromir's behaviour changes in a way that affects this policy — for example if a future version adds crash reporting or an optional cloud backup — we will update this page and change the effective date above before that version is released. Material changes will also be surfaced in the app.
