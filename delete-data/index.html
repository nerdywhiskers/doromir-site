Data deletion

Delete your data

Last updated 4 August 2026

All Doromir data is stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes everything. There is no server copy, because there is no server. You do not need to contact us, submit a request, or wait for anything — deletion is immediate and entirely in your hands.

Export first, if you want to keep anything

Deletion is permanent and we cannot reverse it — we have no copy to restore from. If there is anything in your journal you want to keep, export it before you delete:

  • Open Doromir and go to Profile.
  • Choose the export option and pick a folder to write the file to.
  • Your entries and health snapshots are written out as a file you keep.

Delete a single dream

Open the entry from the Journal tab and delete it. This removes the entry and its audio recording from your device immediately.

Delete everything, keep the app

On Android:

  • Open your device Settings.
  • Go to Apps → Doromir.
  • Tap Storage & cache → Clear storage.

This erases the journal database, every audio recording, your alarms, your settings and any downloaded AI model. The app remains installed and starts fresh, as if newly installed.

Delete everything, including the app

Uninstall Doromir. Android removes the app's entire private storage with it — recordings, transcripts, journal entries, embeddings, health snapshots, alarms, settings and any downloaded model. Nothing survives the uninstall, and nothing is left behind on any server.

Delete a downloaded AI model on its own

If you downloaded the optional on-device language model and want the storage back without losing your journal, you can remove just the model from Doromir's settings. Your entries are unaffected.

Revoke health access

Doromir only ever reads from Health Connect — it never wrote anything to your health store, so there is nothing of ours to remove from it. To stop Doromir reading further:

  • Open Health Connect (or Settings → Security & privacy → More privacy settings → Health Connect).
  • Go to App permissions → Doromir.
  • Remove all permissions.

Health figures already copied into Doromir's local database are deleted when you clear the app's storage or uninstall it, as described above. The same applies to the optional phone-sensor sleep detection, which you can switch off in Doromir's settings or by revoking the physical activity permission.

What we hold about you

Nothing. Doromir has no account system, no cloud sync and no backend service. We do not collect analytics, crash reports, device identifiers or an email address, so there is no record of you on our side to delete and no deletion request for you to file. See the Privacy Policy for the full picture.

The one exception is a file you exported yourself: once you have written that file somewhere or shared it, it is outside the app, and deleting it is up to you.
